Global Tech innovation

WeZe Corporation is a startup engaged in technology and education. Aims to continue to seek innovation for the convenience of human life, as a means of channeling strange but brilliant ideas for each individual and as a place for the development of science. Conceptually, Weze corps is a third-party tool that will bring together idea owners, academic experts and investors. Because I realize that many people have brilliant ideas but cannot process them or someone who has expertise in a field is forced to not be able to maximize the existing potential due to limited funds. Likewise, if an investor is too much concerned with research which of course is out of his scientific field, here Weze Corporation is here to be the middle point of the problem which is in charge of managing the portion of each party properly, one of which is the WeZe Corporation website.
